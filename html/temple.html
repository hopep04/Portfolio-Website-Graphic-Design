TEMPLE

For my UCF class project, I created a Japanese temple environment. I used Maya to model the temple and its surrounding assets, focusing on hard-surface modeling to create the building’s structure and details. I created trim sheets to texture the models and used Photoshop to create alpha textures for the tree. I also used a Simple Grass Wind effect in Unreal Engine to add movement to the plants and make the environment feel more natural.

Tools: Maya, Photoshop, Unreal Engine

Role: Environment Artist

Project: UCF Modeling II
