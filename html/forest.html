FOREST

For my UCF class project, I created a dark and foggy forest environment. I modeled and textured the trees and other environmental assets myself, using Maya and Substance Painter. I then brought everything into Unreal Engine, where I adjusted the lighting to create a gloomy atmosphere. I also used volumetric fog and bloom effects to give the environment a more mysterious appearance.

Tools: Unreal Engine & Substance Painter

Role: Environment Artist

Project: UCF Class Project
