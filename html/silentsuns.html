SILENT SUNS

During my Workshop I class, my group worked on a 3D game called Silent Suns. I was responsible for modeling environmental assets, including rocks, mushrooms, and props for the mine facility. I also created hand-drawn textures in Photoshop and textured 3D models using Substance Painter, including the exterior of the spaceship. Along with modeling and texturing, I created particle effects such as floating particles, mushroom spores, and fog.

Tools: Maya, Substance Painter, Photoshop, Unity

Role: 3D & 2D Artist

Project: UCF Workshop I Class
